# Domain 7: Hive equipment (invented test chapter)
Checked as of 2026-11, a fixture for dev/careers/bank-build.test.js; not course content.

### Queen excluders
A queen excluder is a flat grid fitted between the brood box and the honey boxes. Its gaps are wide enough for a worker
bee and too narrow for the larger queen, so she stays below and lays her eggs only in the brood box. It stays in place
for the whole season while honey boxes are on the hive.

### Bee escape boards
A bee escape board carries a one-way exit. It goes under the honey boxes a day or two before harvest: bees walk down
through the exit and cannot find their way back up, so the boxes are nearly empty of bees when they are lifted off.

### Choosing what goes between the boxes
An excluder sorts bees by size and stays all season. An escape board clears every bee out and is used only before a
harvest. A fume board also clears honey boxes, but within minutes, using a scent the bees avoid.
