## Rulings on the writer's flags
- **PHY.6** (i1-1-17eb1a96.6, fire suppression and a backup generator counted as physical): **upheld.** The chapter lists fire suppression and backup power under physical controls ("Protect the supporting environment"). NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 puts Emergency Power (PE-11) and Fire Protection (PE-13) under Physical and Environmental Protection. The technical option fails because nothing enforces an access or data rule.
- **PRE.5** (i1-1-e1ccd45a.5, patching before exploitation counted as preventive): **upheld.** The chapter states the timing rule: patching is preventive before exploitation and corrective after. The corrective card's example (incident response "patches the hole") agrees, and the stem states the timing plainly.
- **C16 tagging** (wrong options naming Operational, Physical, Corrective, Compensating or Directive tagged with the two overview cards at level 1): **acceptable, no change.** The overview cards (i1-1-7215172d, i1-1-6e4e84fd) define every category and type by name, so a learner at that lesson has already met the term. Tagging the term's own card, taught later, would break C16's fairness rule. The build reports no C16 problem.
