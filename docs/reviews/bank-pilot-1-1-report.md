- **Wrong keys found anywhere: 0.** Blind solvers never picked a different answer than the key; every finding was a
  second defensible reading, a tell, a copied scene or an unsupported phrase.
- **Pass 4 error rate:** 2 / 122 checked = 1.6% (both in why or explain lines).
- **Questions touched after writing:** 23 of 120 (19%); 2 replaced outright.
- **BEST / MOST:** 15 written, 12 kept as BEST (3 had a runner true only of another control in the stem).
- **Agents:** 13 runs (1 writer, 2 rewriters, 5 reviewers, 4 blind solvers, 1 final). No calls on the owner's key.
- **Side fix:** the fact check found the chapter called "logical" and "administrative" OLDER names; NIST uses them as
  current synonyms. Corrected in the study guide (1.71.3).

## Lessons carried into the writer brief for the other objectives
Every scenario names a role; BEST only when the thing asked about has two honest readings; no stem word from the
key's family; options in one grammatical form; no scene reused with names changed; varied definition wording; timing
stated plainly where a type depends on it (preventive before, corrective after).

## Still weak
- About 45 stems written before the role rule name only an organization (they passed review).
- `item` links on a few wrong options point at the overview card rather than the term's own card (learner never sees it).
- One explain says "a stolen certificate" where the stem says the private key was copied (chapter uses the same
  shorthand; passed).
