## Rulings on pass 1's open points
- **BEST on a stem with two controls.** Rule used: BEST and `partly` are right when the thing asked about has two honest readings (a visible camera is detective and deterrent; a firewall in front of an unpatchable device is preventive and compensating; a badge system is physical by the card). They are wrong when the "partly" option is true only of the OTHER control in the stem and not of what the ask names. By that rule: i1-1-02a955cb.5, i1-1-3aa6b30b.5 and i1-1-17eb1a96.5 became plain questions (key, detail and facts unchanged); i1-1-172e8ab8.7 keeps BEST (the card itself calls badge readers physical). i1-1-867b6c50.6 keeps BEST too (training is read as administrative in some framings).
- **Level 2 options tagged with an overview card.** Only i1-1-7215172d.5 has them, and its options are category pairs, so the overview card is the right tag. No re-tag needed. Level 1 overview tags stay as pass 1 ruled (C16).
- **C10 and C13 warnings.** The build printed none for 1.1, before or after the edits.

## Cross-cutting notes (not fixed here)
- **No role in about 45 scenario stems.** Many stems name only an organization ("A stadium installs...", "A law office turns on..."). The organization and the deciding detail are always there, and real exam items also use this form, so these were passed. If the coordinator wants a role in every stem, that is a writer-side rewrite and each one goes back through passes 1 and 2.
- **Definition template across cards.** Ten cards open with "Which of the following describes a X control?" and six with "Which of the following controls is X?". Three asks were varied (3aa6b30b.0, 3ded6b7b.0, 55fcb3b6.0); the rest is the plain exam phrasing and was kept. The select-TWO "list two X controls" items (172e8ab8.9, 3aa6b30b.9, 867b6c50.9, 17eb1a96.9, 55fcb3b6.9) share one pattern across cards; inside each card they are unique, so kept.
- **Length and position.** No length tell found: the only strictly-longest keys are single category or type words ("Operational", "Compensating") that are simply the longest word of their set. Key positions are spread across A to D in every card.
- **For pass 4:** hedged why lines worth a fact check: i1-1-172e8ab8.5 option A ("can be managerial"), i1-1-55fcb3b6.0 option C ("leans deterrent").
