NEW IN EBIKI 1.34 TO 1.36: RAID BOSSES WITH THEIR OWN FIGHTS, RUN SIZE, AND POWERS
==========================================================================

Before you start: open Anki, and make sure your mode has a deck with due cards.
Raid answers are real Anki reviews.


1. EVERY RAID BOSS NOW HAS ITS OWN HEALTH AND HEARTS
-----------------------------------------------------
What changed: bosses no longer get health from how many cards you have due.
Each boss has its own health and nightly heal, and sets how many hearts YOU get
against it. Later bosses are bigger, but you always get more hearts against them
(or they have an ability that protects you).
  Chronos (#1): 30 health, you get 4 hearts   ...   Void (#26): 120 health, you get 9

Try it:
  - Go to Practice. The Daily raid card shows the boss, your hearts against it, and
    "Each new day: your hearts are full again, the boss heals N."
  - Press FIGHT!. The intro card shows your hearts for THIS boss.
  - Fight one run, then press "Stop for now". Come back: the boss keeps its
    wounds and you keep the hearts you have left today.
  - Lose every heart in a run: the boss RALLIES and heals back half the damage
    that run dealt. Your hearts are full again at once, so you can fight again
    right away (as long as you have due cards). No waiting until tomorrow.
  - Each new day your hearts are full again and the boss has healed a little.
  - Hint now shows the first half of each word (the question already gives the
    first letter, so a first-letter hint added nothing).


2. QUESTIONS PER RUN (YOUR CHOICE)
----------------------------------
What changed: a run is a slice of the boss. Pick how many questions a run asks
(5, 10, 15, 20 or 30). The boss's health never changes, so a smaller run just
means more, shorter fights.

Try it (either place):
  - On a raid intro card, open "Fight settings" and pick a number under
    "Questions per run". The run reloads with that many cards.
  - Settings > General > Legends card > "Raid questions per run".
  Then fight: the progress bar shows that many questions.


3. POWERS (RAIDS ONLY)
----------------------
What changed: beating DIFFERENT raid bosses unlocks powers for good. No bag,
no random drops, nothing runs out. Before each fight you choose up to 3 to
bring, and each one you bring works once in that fight.

  Unlocks after beating this many different bosses:
     1  Shield       the next heart you would lose is saved
     3  50:50        leaves 2 choices (typed or choices); works when enraged
     5  Second wind  get back a heart lost this fight
     8  Sharpen      next clean typed answer deals +2
    11  Hint         first half of each word
    13  Bandage      between runs, once a day: the boss doesn't heal tonight
    15  Focus        3 questions: a small slip hits like a clean answer
    17  Siphon       3 questions: every clean answer gives a lost heart back
    19  Ward         used on a boss attack: it can't cost you hearts
    21  Momentum     3 questions: every clean answer crits, crits deal +2
    23  Fury         3 questions: clean typed answers deal double damage
    26  Steadfast    start the fight with 2 extra hearts (lost first)

  Honest Anki: a right answer after a 50:50 or a Hint is saved as Hard. No
  power ever turns a wrong answer right.

Try it the fast way (real reviews still count):
  1. Go to Legends and click the map title 7 times quickly (cheat mode on).
  2. Open "Asset view" in the sidebar > "Raid bosses" tab.
  3. Pick any boss and press "Fight this boss". A test fight brings every power.
  4. Press FIGHT!. Next to Skip, under each question, the power buttons appear
     (a used one gets a check mark and greys out):
       - 50:50 on a typed question: it becomes 2 choices.
       - Hint: the first half of each word shows above the answer box.
       - Shield, then answer wrong on purpose: no heart is lost.
       - Sharpen / Fury / Momentum, then type right answers: bigger hits.
       - Focus, then answer with a small slip: it still hits hard.
       - Siphon after losing a heart, then answer right: the heart comes back.
       - Ward when a boss attack comes: missing it costs nothing.
       - Steadfast: the hearts row starts with 2 extra.
     Every power has its own cast animation, and the window powers show how
     many questions they have left.

Try it for real:
  - Beat Chronos in Practice > Daily raid. The result screen says
    "New power unlocked: Shield!" and how many more bosses unlock the next.
  - On the next raid's intro card, tap the powers to choose up to 3.
  - Once you own a Bandage and the boss survives a run, the result screen and
    the intro card offer "Use a Bandage".


ASK EBI
-------
Ebi's Help knows all of this: open "Talk to Ebi" during a raid and ask
"how many hearts do I get against this boss?" or "what powers have I unlocked?".


4. IMPACT: EVERY RAID BOSS HITS (AND FALLS) ITS OWN WAY (1.36.0)
----------------------------------------------------------------
What changed: the shared fight moments now look different for each of the
26 raid bosses, and the boss's own body moves with them.
  - your hit: a white flash and the boss's own particles (Chronos sheds
    gears, the Vampire bats, the Ratking coins), and the boss reacts in its
    own way (clanks, wobbles, flickers, spins)
  - a critical: a bigger burst + "CRITICAL!"
  - the boss's strike when you miss: its own attack. Chronos sweeps its clock
    hands, the Tempest drops lightning, the Vampire bites, the Reaper swings
    its scythe, the Showman throws cards, the Hydra snaps its jaws...
  - a missed boss attack: the same strike, bigger, + "HEAVY BLOW!"
  - a blocked attack: the strike, smaller, with a parry + "BLOCKED!"
  - the final blow: its own knockout. The Gorgon turns to stone, Moonmaw
    is eclipsed, the Showman's curtain falls, the Void implodes...
  - powers now animate: Shield (a blue dome, then a blue ward on your
    hearts until it is used), 50:50 (a double snip, the 2 tiles pop in),
    Sharpen (a blade glint, then a gold target lock on the boss), Hint
    (golden rays, the hint slides in), Second wind (a heart rises)
  - boss abilities got a pass too: the weakest ones now move more and
    stand out from each other

Try it the fast way: cheat mode on (7 clicks on the Legends map title) > Asset
view > Raid bosses. Under the arena, press each "Impact moments" button and
each "Powers" button. Step through bosses with the arrow keys and compare.

Try it for real: any raid fight. To turn effects off: "Stop animations" on the
fight card, or Settings > General > Legends > Focus mode / Still bosses.
