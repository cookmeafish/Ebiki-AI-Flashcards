NEW IN EBIKI 1.34.0: RAID BOSSES WITH THEIR OWN FIGHTS, RUN SIZE, AND POWERS
==========================================================================

Before you start: open Anki, and make sure your mode has a deck with due cards.
Raid answers are real Anki reviews.


1. EVERY RAID BOSS NOW HAS ITS OWN HEALTH AND HEARTS
-----------------------------------------------------
What changed: bosses no longer get health from how many cards you have due.
Each one has its own health, hearts and nightly heal. Later bosses are bigger,
but always come with more hearts (or an ability that protects you).
  Chronos (#1): 30 health, 4 hearts   ...   Void (#26): 120 health, 9 hearts

Try it:
  - Go to Practice. The Daily raid card shows the boss, its hearts, and
    "Each new day: your hearts are full again, the boss heals N."
  - Press FIGHT!. The intro card shows your hearts for THIS boss.
  - Fight one run, then press "Stop for now". Come back: the boss keeps its
    wounds and you keep the hearts you have left today.
  - Tomorrow your hearts are full again and the boss has healed a little.


2. QUESTIONS PER RUN (YOUR CHOICE)
----------------------------------
What changed: a run is a slice of the boss. Pick how many questions a run asks
(5, 10, 15, 20 or 30). The boss's health never changes, so a smaller run just
means more, shorter fights.

Try it (either place):
  - On a raid intro card, open "Fight settings" and pick a number under
    "Questions per run". The run reloads with that many cards.
  - Settings > General > Legends card > "Raid questions per run".
  Then fight: the progress bar shows that many questions.


3. POWERS (RAIDS ONLY)
----------------------
What changed: beating raid bosses unlocks powers. They drop at random after
runs into a small bag (3 slots, 4 after 13 wins, 5 after 20).

  Unlocks after:  1 win  Shield       the next heart you would lose is saved
                  3 wins 50:50        leaves 2 choices; works even when enraged
                  5 wins Second wind  get back a heart lost this run
                  8 wins Sharpen      next clean typed answer deals +2
                 11 wins Hint         first letter of each word
                 13 wins Bandage      between runs: the boss doesn't heal tonight

  Drops: 1 for finishing a run (10+ questions, or your whole run if shorter),
  1 for 5 clean typed answers in a row, 1 for a win. A rough run (half your
  hearts lost) tends to drop Shield or Second wind.
  Limits: each boss allows 1 to 3 powers per run, one per question.
  Honest Anki: a right answer after a 50:50 or a Hint is saved as Hard.

Try it the fast way (no wins needed, nothing is spent, real reviews still count):
  1. Go to Legends and click the map title 7 times quickly (cheat mode on).
  2. Open "Asset view" in the sidebar > "Raid bosses" tab.
  3. Pick any boss and press "Fight this boss". This test fight gives you one of
     every power.
  4. On the intro you'll see the power bag. Press FIGHT!.
  5. Next to Skip, under each question, the power buttons appear:
       - Press 50:50 on a typed question: it becomes 2 choices. Answer it.
       - Press Hint: the first letters show above the answer box.
       - Press Shield, then answer wrong on purpose: no heart is lost.
       - Press Sharpen, then type a right answer: the boss takes 2 extra.
       - After losing a heart, press Second wind: the heart comes back.
     Watch the purple "Shield up" / "Sharpened" line above the question.
  6. Turn cheat mode off the same way (7 clicks) or in Settings > General.

Try it for real:
  - Beat Chronos in Practice > Daily raid. The result screen shows
    "You found: ..." and how many more wins unlock the next power.
  - Your bag shows on the next raid's intro card.
  - Once you own a Bandage and the boss survives a run, the result screen and
    the intro card offer "Use a Bandage".


ASK EBI
-------
Ebi's Help knows all of this: open "Talk to Ebi" during a raid and ask
"how many hearts does this boss give?" or "what powers do I have?".


4. IMPACT: EVERY RAID FIGHT NOW HITS HARDER (1.35.0)
----------------------------------------------------
What changed: every raid boss reacts with weight, in its own color:
  - your hit: sparks and a shockwave, the arena jolts
  - a critical (every 3rd clean answer in a row): a bigger burst + "CRITICAL!"
  - the boss lands a blow on a miss: claw slashes and a red glow
  - a missed boss attack (costs 2 hearts): heavier slashes + "HEAVY BLOW!"
  - you answer an attack right: a parry + "BLOCKED!"
  - a Shield saves you: a shield bubble + "SAVED!"; Second wind: a heart rises
  - the final blow: the boss shudders, flashes and falls; rays + "DEFEATED!"

Try it the fast way: cheat mode on (7 clicks on the Legends map title) > Asset
view > Raid bosses > under the arena, press each "Impact moments" button.
Step through bosses with the arrow keys: each boss's impact takes its color.

Try it for real: any raid fight. To turn effects off: "Stop animations" on the
fight card, or Settings > General > Legends > Focus mode / Still bosses.
